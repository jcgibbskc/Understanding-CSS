The Box Model

I am the Content.

The space around me is Padding.

The line around that is the Border.

The space outside that is the Margin.

Learning Goals & Observations

  • Padding: Increases the "cushion" inside the box.
  • Border: Acts as the frame; thickness affects total width.
  • Margin: Pushes the box away from other elements.
  • Border-Radius: Softens the corners.
